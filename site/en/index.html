Our mission

“We inspire inner dialogue and self-discovery: gently, with care, and for real”

The Know Yourself retreat gives women a space to find inner support and clarity. Among like-minded women and with a guide by your side, you can pause, hear your own voice and find harmony among the mountains and the silence.

Who the retreat is for

If you recognise yourself in one of these, this is for you.

Women aged 30–40 looking for themselves

When you have achieved a lot on the outside, but inside you want meaning, support and a real “I want” instead of “I must”.

A sense of unfulfilment

When every day feels the same, energy is low and the feeling that life is passing you by won’t go away.

A wish to find your inner compass

When you need a safe space to hear yourself and find clarity without pressure or rush.
